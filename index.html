🐦 Moraine State Park Bird & Nest Guide
Eastern Bluebird (Target Native)
✓ Native Species

Nest: Neat, tidy cup made of fine grasses, pine needles, or weed stems.

Eggs: 4–6 clear sky-blue (occasionally pure white) unspotted eggs.

Incubation / Fledge: 12–14 days incubation; 16–21 days until fledge.

Note: Do NOT open the box after Day 13 of chick life to prevent premature fledging.

Tree Swallow (Native Competitor)
✓ Native Species

Nest: Grass cup copiously lined with soft white or grey feathers.

Eggs: 4–7 pure white, delicate eggs.

Note: Excellent mosquito eaters! If paired with a bluebird box 15-20ft away, both species will nest peacefully side-by-side.

House Wren (Native Competitor)
✓ Native Protected

Nest: Fills entire box almost to the hole with small coarse twigs, lined with feathers or hair.

Eggs: 5–8 tiny pinkish-white eggs heavily speckled with cinnamon brown spots.

Note: Wrens are legally protected under the Migratory Bird Treaty Act. Once they lay an egg, do NOT remove.

Black-capped Chickadee
✓ Native Species

Nest: Thick soft base of green moss lined with rabbit fur or down.

Eggs: 5–8 white eggs with reddish-brown speckles concentrated at the larger end.

House Sparrow (Invasive Pest)
⚠️ Non-Native / Invasive

Nest: Messy domed pile of coarse grass, paper scraps, straw, and cigarette butts with a side entrance.

Eggs: 4–7 cream or dull white eggs heavily blotched with grey and brown.

Action: House Sparrows attack and kill native bluebird adults and chicks. In Pennsylvania, House Sparrows are not protected — their nests and eggs should be completely removed and discarded.
